# Closeout logo decision

> **Decision status:** Approved by the founder on 2026-07-22.  
> **Selected direction:** Concept C — The Keystone Fold.  
> **Public brand:** Closeout · **Domain:** `closeoutflow.com`.

## Founder decision

Concept C, **The Keystone Fold**, is the final Closeout logo direction. This decision closes the Phase 5E-B founder checkpoint and authorizes the selected mark to replace the temporary “C” tile across the application, authentication experience, browser/PWA assets, social and email assets.

The selected direction is not a license to ship the exploration mark unchanged. The final mark must retain the structural character of Concept C while making verified completion and handoff clearer and avoiding a generic block-C, hexagonal monogram, finance chevron, cryptocurrency mark, or Autodesk-like angular construction.

## Founder geometry clarification

On 2026-07-22, the founder supplied the original visual reference again and confirmed that the final logo must retain its compact, filled, faceted C-frame. The stroke-built spine and check construction was rejected because it read as a capital P in authentication, sidebar, inverse, and small-header contexts.

The selected production refinement is **Balanced Aperture**, the closest reconstruction of the supplied reference. Its construction uses:

1. A compact filled frame with chamfered outer corners.
2. A centered angular aperture that remains visibly open on the right.
3. Balanced inward terminal folds that imply closure around a deliberate handoff point.
4. Subtle planar facets at display sizes, with a flat optical cut at favicon sizes.

The shape preserves a subtle connection to Closeout without relying on a literal typeset letter, extended vertical stem, bowl, or checkmark construction.

## Small-size behavior

The 16×16, 32×32, and compact shell marks use a dedicated simplified cut:

- the planar facet overlays are removed;
- the center aperture is opened slightly;
- the paired terminals are shortened proportionally;
- the filled silhouette remains intact.

The compact asset is a redrawn reduction, not the detailed symbol scaled down. The symbol must remain recognizable without the wordmark and without relying on color.

## Lockup and variants

- **Primary lockup:** engineered-blue symbol, graphite Closeout wordmark, 14px visual gap at the standard application size.
- **Compact lockup:** simplified symbol, 10px visual gap, smaller wordmark for constrained headers and email.
- **Dark/inverse:** identical geometry in white or light ink; no glow, gradient, or alternate silhouette.
- **Monochrome:** single graphite/black ink with the same geometry.
- **Wordmark:** IBM Plex Sans, semibold, restrained negative tracking; visible name is always **Closeout**.

## Usage boundaries

- Do not place the mark inside a generic rounded-square tile except where the operating system requires an app-icon canvas.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, outlines, or decorative animation to the mark.
- Do not reintroduce the temporary “C” tile.
- Do not use the detailed fold seam below 32px.
- Do not alter the public name to “CloseoutFlow” or “Closeout Flow.” Internal technical identifiers remain unchanged.
- Formal trademark clearance remains a founder/legal step before broad commercial registration or paid brand campaigns.

## Phase authorization
